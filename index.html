01 — About

Offensive mindset, builder's hands.

A short introduction to who I am, what I study and the kind of work I enjoy most.

I'm Ankit Kumar, a BCA Cyber Security Enthusiast and Full-Stack Developer from New Delhi, India. My curiosity started with a simple question — "How does this actually break?" — and that question turned into a daily habit of building applications and then auditing them like an attacker would.

On the defensive side I practise Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing (VAPT), Web Application Security and the OWASP Top 10. On the building side I ship responsive, accessible interfaces with vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and back them with Node and Python. I live in the terminal — Linux is my daily driver and Bash is my duct tape.

I'm currently looking for internships, junior security roles and freelance web projects where I can keep learning from people who are better than me. Outside of that you'll find me on CTF platforms, reading disclosure write-ups, or shipping small tools to GitHub.
